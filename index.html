How privacy is protected

Your browser computes a SHA-1 hash. Only its first five hexadecimal characters are sent to the service. The returned hash suffixes are compared locally.

A password not found in this database is not guaranteed safe. A positive result does not identify which account was affected.

03 / LEARN

Build better password habits

01

Go for length

Prefer long, unique passwords or passphrases. Length matters, but predictability matters too.

02

Never reuse

Give every account its own password to limit damage if one service is breached.

03

Use a password manager

A reputable password manager can generate and store unique passwords.

04

Enable MFA

Multi-factor authentication adds another layer of protection to your accounts.
